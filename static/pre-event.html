Pre-Event: vLLM Inference Meetup – Delhi

An official pre-event of the Agentic AI Workshop 2026 at IIT Delhi.

About the meetup

AI inference at scale

Architecture, performance optimizations, and raw engineering challenges.

Join the vLLM and llm-d AI Inference Meetup in Delhi for a technical exploration of the architecture, performance optimizations, and engineering challenges involved in running AI inference at scale.

The meetup brings together developers, AI/ML engineers, infrastructure specialists, and researchers interested in efficient inference systems and deploying large language models in production.

What to expect

Four core themes

Practical insights from engineering teams building state-of-the-art inference engines.

Deep Technical Sessions

Learn from experts about AI inference architectures, optimization techniques, and the vLLM and llm-d ecosystems.

Scaling AI in Production

Explore the engineering challenges of deploying and operating large language model inference systems at scale.

Hands-On Learning

Discover practical approaches to efficient inference, serving, and infrastructure optimization.

Networking

Connect with developers, researchers, engineers, and industry professionals working on AI inference.

Target audience

Who should attend?

Engineers, researchers, and systems practitioners working across the inference stack.

vLLM & llm-d Community

Users, contributors, and developers leveraging vLLM and llm-d for high-throughput LLM serving.

ML & Systems Engineers

Machine learning and infrastructure engineers working on inference pipelines, latency reduction, and model serving.

Platform & Production Teams

Platform and MLOps teams deploying and operating generative AI workloads in production environments.

Researchers & Developers

Researchers and software developers interested in efficient inference across local machines, cloud clusters, and Kubernetes.

Event agenda

Technical program overview

A focused day of in-depth sessions and hands-on instruction.

01 Keynote

Opening Technical Keynote

An opening technical keynote introducing the state of high-throughput model serving, architectural foundations, and inference innovations.

04 Talks

Technical Talks

Four in-depth technical sessions exploring AI inference engineering, architecture, memory management, and performance optimization.

01 Lab

Hands-On Lab

Practical learning focused on hands-on deployment, tuning, and benchmarking of AI inference workloads.

The detailed agenda and speaker information will be announced by the event organisers.

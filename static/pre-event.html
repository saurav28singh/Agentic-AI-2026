Pre-Event: vLLM Inference Meetup – Delhi

A technical meetup focused on efficient AI inference, vLLM, llm-d, and deploying large language models at scale.

View Event Details & Register →
14 Dec 2026
1:30 PM – 5:00 PM
IIT Delhi · Yardi School of Artificial Intelligence – Computer Lab

Highlights

What to expect

Practical insights from engineering teams building state-of-the-art inference engines.

Technical Sessions

Explore AI inference architectures, memory optimization, and the vLLM and llm-d serving ecosystems.

Hands-On Learning

Discover practical approaches to high-throughput model serving, low-latency deployment, and infrastructure tuning.

Networking

Connect with developers, AI/ML engineers, and systems researchers working on production AI inference.

Target audience

Who should attend

This meetup is designed for AI/ML engineers, systems researchers, platform and MLOps teams, and software developers interested in high-throughput LLM serving, inference latency reduction, and running large models efficiently across local machines, cloud clusters, and Kubernetes environments.

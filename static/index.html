THEMES

Exploring the Frontiers of Agentic AI

Talks, tutorials and hackathon tracks are organised around three themes.

Agentic AI in Healthcare

Agents that support clinicians and patients through triage, medical record summarisation, care coordination, and faster diagnostics — with safety, fairness, and accountability.

Agentic AI for Scientific Discovery

AI agents that explore research literature, generate hypotheses, design experiments, analyse results, and accelerate scientific discovery.

Agentic AI and Enterprise

From prototypes to products: building, funding, and scaling agentic AI ventures, alongside the business models that autonomous agents make possible.

FAQs

Who can attend?

Researchers, faculty, students, industry practitioners, startups and investors working on or around agentic AI. Places are limited, so every registration is reviewed and confirmed by the organising committee.

Is there a registration fee?

There is no registration charge for the workshop. If any fees are introduced for particular categories, they will be announced on this website.

Will I know my registration went through?

Yes. Submitting the form sends you a copy of your responses immediately, and a separate e-mail from the committee confirms whether a place is available.

Can I present my work?

We welcome posters presenting work on Agentic AI, including arXiv preprints and papers currently under submission.

What should I bring to the tutorials?

A laptop for most of them. Software and account prerequisites are published with each session once the line-up is final.

How do I get to campus?

IIT Delhi is in Hauz Khas, South Delhi, about 15 km from the airport and a short ride from the IIT and Hauz Khas metro stations. See venue and travel.

THEMES

Exploring the Frontiers of Agentic AI

Talks, tutorials and hackathon tracks are organised around three themes.

Agentic AI in Healthcare

Agents that support clinicians and patients through triage, medical record summarisation, care coordination, and faster diagnostics — with safety, fairness, and accountability.

Agentic AI for Scientific Discovery

AI agents that explore research literature, generate hypotheses, design experiments, analyse results, and accelerate scientific discovery.

Agentic AI for Enterprise

From prototypes to products: building, funding, and scaling agentic AI ventures, alongside the business models that autonomous agents make possible.

FAQs

Who Can Attend the Workshop?

The workshop welcomes researchers, faculty members, students, industry professionals, entrepreneurs, and anyone interested in agentic AI and its applications in healthcare, scientific discovery, and entrepreneurship.

Is There a Registration Fee?

No, participation in the workshop is free of charge. However, registration is required, and attendance is subject to confirmation by the organising committee.

How Will I Know If My Registration Is Confirmed?

You will receive an acknowledgement after submitting the registration form. Further details regarding your registration status and participation will be communicated by the organising committee.

Can I Present My Research or Work at the Workshop?

Yes. Participants are welcome to submit their relevant research and project work for consideration. Accepted papers, arXiv preprints, and papers currently under review are welcome. Presentation opportunities and submission guidelines will be communicated through the official workshop channels.

What Should I Bring to the Tutorials?

Please bring your laptop and ensure that it is fully charged. Any additional software, tools, datasets, or setup instructions will be shared by the tutorial organisers, if required.

How Can I Reach the IIT Delhi Campus?

The workshop will take place at IIT Delhi, Hauz Khas, New Delhi. Participants can reach the campus by metro, taxi, or other local transport. Detailed venue and entry instructions will be shared with registered participants before the event.

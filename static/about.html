Why agentic AI?

Where autonomous AI agents meet healthcare, science and entrepreneurship.

AI systems are moving from answering questions to taking actions: planning multi-step tasks, using tools, collaborating with people and with other agents, and learning from feedback. The shift opens real opportunities and raises hard questions about reliability, safety and accountability.

The Agentic AI Workshop 2026 brings researchers, practitioners, founders and investors to IIT Delhi to take stock of where agentic AI stands, where it is heading, and how it can create real impact in healthcare, scientific discovery and enterprice.

Agentic AI describes systems that pursue goals with a degree of autonomy. Rather than producing a single response, an agent runs a loop, usually with a large language model as its reasoning engine, combined with memory, tool use and human oversight.

What is an agentic system?

Plan, delegate, verify, repeat

An agentic system brings together specialized AI agents that collaborate, exchange information, and iteratively refine their outputs to accomplish complex tasks.

An orchestrator coordinates specialised agents for retrieval, reasoning, and verification through shared memory. If verification fails, it re-plans instead of repeating the same attempt. However, reliability, evaluation, tool safety, and human oversight are essential for deploying trustworthy agentic systems.

Routes & Access

Getting to campus

Travel times are approximate and depend heavily on traffic.

From the airport
Indira Gandhi International (DEL) is about 15 km away, roughly 30–60 minutes by taxi. Prepaid taxis and app cabs are at every terminal. The Magenta Line runs from Terminal 1 and the Airport Express from Terminal 3.
From the stations
New Delhi and Hazrat Nizamuddin are 12–16 km away, roughly 40–60 minutes by taxi. From New Delhi station the Yellow Line runs directly to Hauz Khas.
By metro
Nearest stations are IIT (Magenta Line) and Hauz Khas (Yellow and Magenta Lines). From either, a short auto-rickshaw or cab ride reaches campus. The DMRC journey planner gives the best route.
By road
The campus sits in Hauz Khas, South Delhi. App cabs and autos are widely available; allow extra time at peak hours. Visitor parking on campus is limited.
Campus entry
IIT Delhi is a secured campus. Bring a valid government photo ID and the registration ID e-mailed to you. The exact hall, entry gate and visitor-pass details are posted here and e-mailed before the workshop.

About the workshop

Why agentic AI, and why now?

Where autonomous AI agents meet healthcare, science and entrepreneurship.

Why this workshop

From answering to acting

The shift that makes this the right moment.

AI systems are moving from answering questions to taking actions: planning multi-step tasks, using tools, collaborating with people and with other agents, and learning from feedback. The shift opens real opportunities and raises hard questions about reliability, safety and accountability.

The Agentic AI Workshop 2026 brings researchers, practitioners, founders and investors to IIT Delhi to take stock of where agentic AI stands, where it is heading, and how it can create real impact in healthcare, scientific discovery and entrepreneurship.

Agentic AI describes systems that pursue goals with a degree of autonomy. Rather than producing a single response, an agent runs a loop, usually with a large language model as its reasoning engine, combined with memory, tool use and human oversight.

  1. 01

    Perceive

    Takes in the goal and its surroundings: requests, documents, sensor data, tool output.

  2. 02

    Reason

    Works out what the goal requires and what it still needs to know.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Breaks the goal into a sequence of steps it can carry out.

  4. 04

    Act

    Calls tools and APIs, writes to systems, or delegates to other agents.

  5. 05

    Reflect

    Checks the outcome against the goal and revises before trying again.

Worked example

Which papers since 2024 report agents running wet-lab experiments?
  1. 01orchestratorSplits the question into retrieve → draft → verify, and sets the rule that every claim carries a citation.planned
  2. 02retrieverQueries arXiv, PubMed and bioRxiv, de-duplicates and ranks what comes back.38 hits
  3. 03reasonerDrafts a six-paragraph summary, each claim tagged to the source it came from.drafted
  4. 04verifierRe-reads every cited paper against the claim made about it. Two claims are not supported by their source.2 failed
  5. 05orchestratorKeeps the eighteen claims that held. Re-queries only the two that did not, instead of starting again.revise
  6. 06retrieverFinds a genuine source for one claim. For the other there is nothing to find.1 of 2
  7. 07reasonerRewrites one paragraph and drops the claim that nothing supports.redrafted
  8. 08verifierEvery remaining claim now matches its source.passed
  9. 09orchestratorReturns the summary, the citations, and a note saying what was dropped and why.done

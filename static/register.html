You get an acknowledgement straight away

A copy of your responses arrives by e-mail the moment you submit, so you have a record of what you entered. If it does not appear within a few minutes, check your spam folder before writing to us.

Posters

Bring your research

The poster session runs over lunch on Day 1.

The poster session is open to arXiv preprints and papers currently under submission, on the broader theme of agentic AI. To present, answer “Yes” to the poster question in the registration form and give your title and abstract.

Submit a poster

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else, write to the organisers.

Who can attend?

Researchers, faculty, students, industry practitioners, startups and investors working on or around agentic AI. Places are limited, so registrations are reviewed and confirmed by the organising committee.

Is there a registration fee?

Attendance is free. If any fees are introduced for particular categories, they will be announced on this page.

Will I know my registration went through?

Yes. Submitting the form sends you a copy of your responses immediately. A separate e-mail from the committee confirms whether a place is available.

When will the detailed schedule be available?

The draft schedule is already on the programme page. Session titles and speakers are filled in as they confirm, and registered attendees get the final version by e-mail.

What should I bring to the tutorials?

A laptop for most of them. Software and account prerequisites are published with each tutorial once the line-up is final.

Will the talks be recorded?

Selected slides and recordings may be shared on this site after the event, with each speaker's permission.

Is accommodation provided?

Not unless announced otherwise. The venue page lists areas close to campus, and a limited number of guest-house rooms may be available on request.

How can my company exhibit or sponsor?

See partners and sponsors for the tiers, or write to the organisers and we will talk it through.

Formats

Six kinds of session

Six kinds of session run across the two days.

Keynote

Big-picture talks from leading voices in agentic AI.

Talk

Research and industry talks on agents at work.

Panel

Academia and industry on where agents are heading.

Tutorial

Hands-on sessions where you build and run agents yourself. Bring a laptop.

Demo

Live agents working on real problems, from labs and companies.

Kiosks

Meet the companies building agents

Industry kiosks run alongside the sessions on both days.

Kiosks give companies and startups a space to showcase agentic AI products, research prototypes and career opportunities to a highly engaged audience.

Routes

Getting to campus

Travel times are approximate and depend heavily on traffic.

From the airport
Indira Gandhi International (DEL) is about 15 km away, roughly 30–60 minutes by taxi. Prepaid taxis and app cabs are at every terminal. The Magenta Line runs from Terminal 1 and the Airport Express from Terminal 3.
From the stations
New Delhi and Hazrat Nizamuddin are 12–16 km away, roughly 40–60 minutes by taxi. From New Delhi station the Yellow Line runs directly to Hauz Khas.
By metro
Nearest stations are IIT (Magenta Line) and Hauz Khas (Yellow and Magenta Lines). From either, a short auto-rickshaw or cab ride reaches campus. The DMRC journey planner gives the best route.
By road
The campus sits in Hauz Khas, South Delhi. App cabs and autos are widely available; allow extra time at peak hours. Visitor parking on campus is limited.
Campus entry
IIT Delhi is a secured campus. Bring a valid government photo ID and the registration ID e-mailed to you. The exact hall, entry gate and visitor-pass details are posted here and e-mailed before the workshop.

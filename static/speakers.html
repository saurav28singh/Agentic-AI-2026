Distinguished Speakers

Prof. Surya Ganguli

Associate Professor of Applied Physics

Stanford University

Bridges theoretical physics, neuroscience, and machine learning at Stanford. His research investigates the mathematical foundations of deep neural networks, neural dynamics, and scaling laws to understand biological and artificial intelligence.

Prof. Manik Varma

Technical Fellow & Corporate Vice President

Microsoft Research India

Technical Fellow at Microsoft Research India and Adjunct Professor at IIT Delhi. He pioneered extreme multi-label classification, developing scalable machine learning algorithms widely deployed in global web search and recommendation systems.

Dr. Nanda Kishore Kambhatla

Head of Adobe Research Lab

Adobe Research, India

Heads Adobe Research India, driving research in natural language processing, conversational AI, and content intelligence. He previously led machine learning and dialogue systems initiatives at IBM Research for IBM Watson.

Dr. Balaji Vasan Srinivasan

Principal Scientist

Adobe Research, India

Principal Scientist at Adobe Research India specializing in multimodal artificial intelligence, generative models, and computer vision. His work advances text-to-image synthesis, automated layout generation, and AI-assisted creative workflows.

Dr. Prashant Warier

Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Qure.ai

Co-Founder and CEO of Qure.ai and an IIT Delhi alumnus. He develops deep learning diagnostic algorithms for medical imaging, deploying automated screening solutions for tuberculosis, stroke, and lung cancer across over 100 countries.

Dr. Arijit Patra

Senior Principal Scientist

UCB Pharma

Senior Principal Scientist at UCB Pharma and Rhodes Scholar from Oxford. He focuses on artificial intelligence for biomedical imaging, non-clinical drug safety workflows, and machine learning pipelines that accelerate therapeutic discovery.

Dr. Nitish Naik

Professor of Cardiology

AIIMS, New Delhi

Professor of Cardiology at AIIMS, New Delhi, and Padma Shri recipient. His work encompasses interventional cardiology, cardiovascular diagnostics, and the rigorous, clinically validated translation of artificial intelligence technologies into patient care.

Prof. Karthik Raman

Professor, Data Science and AI, and Biotechnology

IIT Madras

Professor at IIT Madras across Data Science and Biotechnology, and coordinator of IBSE. His research integrates computational systems biology, metabolic modeling, and biological network analysis to investigate complex disease mechanisms.

Prof. Raghunathan Rengaswamy

Professor, Data Science and AI, and Chemical Engineering

IIT Madras

Marti Mannariah Gurunath Chair Professor at IIT Madras. His research focuses on process systems engineering, fault detection and diagnosis, reinforcement learning, and combining first-principles physical models with data-driven analytics.

Dr. Deepak N. Subramani

Associate Professor, Computational and Data Sciences

IISc Bangalore

Associate Professor at IISc Bangalore and MIT alumnus leading the QUEST Lab. His research focuses on physics-informed machine learning, autonomous robotic navigation, ocean forecasting, and Bayesian uncertainty quantification.

Prof. Ashutosh Modi

Associate Professor, Computer Science and Engineering

IIT Kanpur

Associate Professor of Computer Science at IIT Kanpur. He specializes in natural language processing, affective computing, multimodal dialogue systems, and legal AI, developing models for machine comprehension and emotion recognition.

Aparna Taneja

Senior Research Scientist

Google DeepMind

Senior Research Scientist at Google DeepMind India. She leads research in artificial intelligence for social impact, developing restless multi-armed bandits and predictive machine learning models to improve maternal and public health.

Dinesh Raghu

Senior Researcher and Manager, Agentic AI

IBM

Senior Researcher and Manager in Agentic AI at IBM Research India. He leads research on autonomous agents, function calling in IBM Granite foundation models, multi-turn reasoning, and task-oriented conversational workflows.

Nitendra Rajput

Vice President and Head of AI Garage Center

Mastercard

Vice President and Head of the AI Garage at Mastercard, and ACM Distinguished Scientist. He directs global AI research and deployment in financial security, fraud prevention, risk mitigation, and emerging agentic commerce.
